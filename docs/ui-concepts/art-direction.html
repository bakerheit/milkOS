Hand-off

Deliver masters and the exact sizes with a contact sheet (all icons at 48 and 16, light and dark). Wallpapers must pass art/check-wallpaper.py. Sounds follow the table in ART.md and must pass wav_check.py. The UI lead reviews the contact sheet before engineers embed anything.
