1. A notification arrives

Toast, then it settles in the drawer

easy

When

A message lands while you work.

What you see

A toast (264 by 64) slides in top right and stays 5 seconds, pausing while the pointer is on it. The menu bar bell and the app's dock icon pick up a count.

Detail

Clicking the toast opens the drawer on that item. Ignore it and it is waiting in the drawer.

Build note

Toast and badge only. Reuses the toast and glass panels already in the spec.

Main elements

  • Toast (264x64)
  • Bell count
  • Dock badge
  • 5 second timer
  • Pause on hover

Font note

Keep the toast title to about 10 characters and the body to about 22.

2 Drawer open: drawn 1:1 at 1024×768 and scaled to fit.

2. Drawer open

The chosen look

easy

When

Click the bell or press Ctrl-N.

What you see

Grouped cards, filter chips, today's events and Do Not Disturb. Esc or a click outside closes it.

Detail

Selected card has the straw outline.

Build note

One glass panel and a list of cards.

Main elements

  • Header and chips
  • Groups
  • Cards
  • Today card
  • Do Not Disturb

Font note

Bodies are cut with a tilde around 30 characters.

3 Working in the drawer: drawn 1:1 at 1024×768 and scaled to fit.

3. Working in the drawer

Groups, keys and dismissing

easy

When

Arrow keys move through the list.

What you see

A group is expanded to show all four messages, the selected card shows a small x on hover, and the keys are listed (Return opens the source, Backspace dismisses, Left and Right collapse a group).

Detail

Everything works from the keyboard first, with the pointer as a bonus.

Build note

A selection index, a collapsed flag per group and a delete.

Main elements

  • Expandable groups
  • Selection outline
  • Hover x
  • Key map

Font note

Group arrows are plain characters, no icons needed.

4 All caught up: drawn 1:1 at 1024×768 and scaled to fit.

4. All caught up

Empty state with Do Not Disturb on

easy

When

Nothing is waiting.

What you see

A calm check mark, today's events, and Do Not Disturb switched on until 12:00 (lavender row, moon on the bell). Toasts and sounds are held, but cards still collect.

Detail

A quiet drawer feels like a reward, not an empty box.

Build note

One empty-state layout and a timer for Do Not Disturb.

Main elements

  • Empty state
  • Today card
  • DND row
  • Moon on the bell

Font note

The check is a plain character at 2x.
