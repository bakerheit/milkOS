The TLS root store (mozilla-roots.der, listed in mozilla-roots.txt)
====================================================================

mozilla-roots.der holds the root certificates of Mozilla's CA Certificate Program: the
built-in roots of NSS (security/nss/lib/ckfw/builtins/certdata.txt in Mozilla's source tree)
that Mozilla trusts to identify websites. They were extracted from certdata.txt by curl's
mk-ca-bundle.pl (https://curl.se/docs/caextract.html) and converted from PEM to DER, without
other changes, by scripts/gen_roots.py, which records the bundle's date and SHA-256.
